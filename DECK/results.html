How well do models edit 3D artifacts?

Fourteen models edit the same programs across six difficulty levels. Each edit is executed, rendered from six views, and must pass every checklist item.

All-pass rate by model and level

Single-shot editing, 64 tasks per level. Even the best model solves fewer than two thirds of the tasks, and every model loses most of its accuracy once an edit goes beyond a single parameter.

The L1 → L2 cliff, and the open–closed gap
Mean all-pass rate per level. Open-weight models reach near zero from L4 on.
Strongest models per family
All-pass rate per level for the best model of each group.

Why edits fail

Outcome of every edit (14 models × 64 tasks per level). Most failures come from not making the requested change; breaking unrelated parts alone is rare. Programs that crash or render nothing grow with difficulty.

Among rendered edits that fail, 97.7% miss at least one edit item and only 2.3% fail solely on the preservation item. The most common crashes are syntax errors (34%) and undefined attributes or names (30%).
